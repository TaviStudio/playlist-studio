About YouTube playback

YouTube playback requires an internet connection and a visible player. Background playback is available for local audio, but not for YouTube videos. Some videos must open in the YouTube app or website because of YouTube or uploader restrictions.

Why does a YouTube video open outside the app?

The uploader may have disabled embedded playback, the video may be marked as made for kids, or YouTube may otherwise restrict the video. In those cases, Playlist Studio offers to open the official YouTube app or website.

Why does YouTube playback stop in the background?

YouTube playback uses the official embedded player and stops when the app is no longer visible. Return to Playlist Studio to resume it. Imported audio files and personal recordings can continue playing in the background.

Does Playlist Studio download YouTube audio or video?

No. Playlist Studio saves the link and uses YouTube’s official player. It does not download, convert, extract, or create offline copies of YouTube content.

How do I restore Unlimited Library?

Open the Unlimited Library screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that made the original purchase. The purchase is non-consumable and does not renew.

Where are my songs and playlists stored?

Your playlist data, imported copies, and recordings are stored in Playlist Studio’s private app storage on your device. Deleting the app normally removes its local container, although a device or iCloud backup may retain a copy according to your Apple backup settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual songs or playlists inside the app. To remove the entire local library, delete Playlist Studio from the device and manage any device or iCloud backups in Apple’s settings. You can also revoke microphone access in System Settings and disable in-app YouTube playback from Playlist Studio’s Privacy screen.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Playlist Studio: Music Hub and this website handle information.

Summary Data YouTube Apple Your controls Your rights

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Playlist Studio: Music Hub (“Playlist Studio,” “the app,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is provided by Tavi Studio. This policy covers the Playlist Studio app for iPhone and iPad, this support website, and support communications.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to tavistudio@yahoo.com .

At a glance: no account is required. Playlist Studio does not send your imported audio files, recordings, playlists, or library contents to the developer. The app has no developer-operated analytics, advertising, or tracking service.

2. Information processed on your device

The following information may be created, selected, or stored locally in the app’s private container:

  • playlist names, song titles, playlist order, search and library organization;
  • YouTube URLs or video identifiers and the titles you save;
  • audio files you import and local copies created by the app;
  • audio recordings you make in the app;
  • app preferences, including your in-app YouTube playback choice; and
  • local purchase entitlement status needed to unlock Unlimited Library.

This local information is used to provide the library, playlist, playback, recording, import, and purchase features you request. We do not operate a server that receives a copy of your library.

Files

You choose files through Apple’s system file picker. Playlist Studio accesses only the items you select. Selected audio may be converted on the device to M4A/AAC and copied into the app’s private storage so it can be organized and played. The app does not scan unrelated files.

Microphone

Playlist Studio requests microphone permission only when you use the recording feature. Audio is captured, processed, and stored locally. You can deny or revoke access at any time in System Settings. If permission is denied, recording will not work, but the rest of the app remains available.

Background audio and media controls

For local audio playback, Playlist Studio may provide title, playback status, progress, and artwork to Apple’s system media services so that Lock Screen, Control Center, headset, and vehicle controls can work. This processing occurs through iOS or iPadOS.

3. YouTube API Services and embedded playback

Playlist Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using the app’s YouTube features, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service . Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy . You may also review the YouTube API Services Terms of Service .

Information sent to and received from YouTube

When you add a YouTube link, Playlist Studio sends the video identifier to the YouTube Data API to retrieve information such as the video title, channel information, thumbnail, embedding status, and whether the video is marked as made for kids. The app uses this information to verify the link, suggest a formatted artist and song title, and decide whether in-app playback is permitted.

When you allow and use in-app playback, Playlist Studio loads YouTube’s official IFrame Player in a web view. Google or YouTube may receive information normally involved in an internet and embedded-player request, including your IP address, device and browser characteristics, app or request identifiers, cookies or similar technologies, video identifiers, and player interactions or playback information. YouTube may display its own content, branding, recommendations, or advertising according to its policies and your Google or YouTube settings.

Playlist Studio does not use Google or YouTube OAuth, does not ask you to sign in to a Google account, and does not access your Google profile, subscriptions, watch history, private videos, or other authorized account data.

Consent and control

Before embedded YouTube playback, the app asks for your choice. You can disable Allow in-app YouTube playback at any time on the Privacy screen. When disabled, new embedded playback is stopped and YouTube links open only in the official YouTube app or website. Metadata requests needed to identify and validate a YouTube link may still occur when you choose to add such a link.

Playlist Studio does not download, convert, extract audio from, or cache YouTube content for offline playback. Video availability and playback are controlled by YouTube, the uploader, your region, network conditions, and applicable YouTube restrictions.

Unlimited Library is a non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process your Apple ID, payment information, transaction details, device information, storefront, and purchase history under the Apple Privacy Policy . Playlist Studio receives product, transaction, and entitlement information needed to offer, complete, and restore the purchase. We do not receive your complete card number or Apple ID password.

The displayed price is supplied by Apple and can vary by country, currency, tax, and storefront. Restoring a purchase requires the Apple ID used for the original transaction.
