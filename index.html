What can I add to my Library?

Use Import Audio for audio from Files, Import Video from Files or Import Video from Gallery for compatible videos, or Record Audio to make a recording. Voice-over mixes saved in Studio also appear in Library. Add saved items to playlists using Add from Library.

How do I start a Voice-over?

Tap the filled microphone on the home screen, or choose Voice-over from an item menu in Library or a playlist. Before recording, use the speaker in the playback bar if you want to mute the original sound. Tap the microphone to start recording and tap it again to stop. The speaker remains available during recording. When you listen to the mix, adjust Voice and Original separately; Mute/Unmute is now beside the Original slider. The slider stays active, and moving it above zero unmutes the original track without muting your recorded voice.

Will Voice-over replace my original?

No. Studio works with its own copy of the source and saves the finished mix as a separate Library item. Existing original items remain in Library. If you started from a playlist, the saved mix is also added to that playlist while it still exists. Importing a source directly into Studio does not automatically add that original to Library.

What is the difference between Save and Export in Studio?

Save adds or updates the finished mix in Library and keeps the editable session in Recent Sessions on this device. Saving does not use a free export. Export renders the mix with the current settings and opens the share sheet; it does not save those edits to Library. Saved sessions remain editable locally until removed through the session or Library deletion controls.

How do I export an item from Library or a playlist?

Open the item menu and choose Export. Save to Files or choose another destination in the native iOS share sheet. This exports the last saved file without reconverting it. Exporting a Voice-over mix uses the same free allowance as exporting from Studio; ordinary audio, video, and recording exports do not use that allowance.

What is included for free?

Create unlimited playlists, keep up to 25 current items in Library, and complete 10 Voice-over exports on this device. Library items include imported media, recordings, and saved mixes. The limits are independent: after 10 exports you can still use Library within its limit; at 25 Library items you can still use any remaining Studio exports. Lifetime Pro removes both limits through a one-time purchase, subject to device storage and normal technical limits.

What counts toward the 10 free Voice-over exports?

A successful Voice-over export from Studio, Library, or a playlist counts once when the system confirms completion. A further export of the same mix also counts. Previewing or saving a mix to Library, cancelling a share, or a failed export does not count. Deleting a mix does not reset the count. Export usage is stored locally and is separate from iCloud Library.

How do I restore Lifetime Pro?

Open Settings > Lifetime Pro and choose Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account that made the purchase and an internet connection. This one-time purchase does not renew. Restoring the purchase is separate from restoring your files through iCloud.

Can I import from Gallery without giving access to all my videos?

Yes. Import Video from Gallery opens Apple’s system picker and gives Playlist Studio access to the videos you select. The app does not request access to your entire photo library. A video stored only in iCloud Photos may need to download first.

Does Playlist Studio work without internet?

Yes. Import media already available on your device, record audio, create Voice-over mixes, organize playlists, and play locally saved media offline. iCloud sync and purchasing or restoring Lifetime Pro need internet. Media stored only with a cloud provider or in iCloud Photos must first be downloaded. An export destination may also require a connection.

Which video files can I import?

Compatible MP4, MOV, and M4V files from Files or Gallery. Playback depends on the file’s codec and support on your device. Imported video is copied into private app storage without re-encoding. Protected or unsupported files cannot be imported.

Can audio and video play in the background?

Local audio, recordings, saved mixes, and the sound from local videos can continue playing in the background with Lock Screen controls. Compatible videos support Picture in Picture in the regular player on supported devices. Voice-over recording is separate: it stops when the app becomes inactive, and Studio preview does not use Picture in Picture.

How does Repeat work?

Playlists start over after the last item. You can also repeat the current audio, video, recording, or saved Voice-over mix. A playlist or Library with a single item repeats it automatically.

Where are my files and sessions stored?

Imported copies, recordings, saved Voice-over mixes, playlists, and editable Studio sessions are stored on your device. With Sync with iCloud enabled and available, Library items and playlists also sync to private iCloud storage. Recent Sessions, appearance and playback preferences, and the free export count remain local. An editable session is separate from its saved Library mix.

What does the iCloud button mean?

Tap the cloud next to Settings to open iCloud Library. Blue means sync is on; a filled blue cloud means the app has confirmed all known current changes are synced. A gray crossed cloud means sync is off. Check the status inside iCloud Library for connection and transfer details.

How do I enable iCloud sync?

Sync is off on first use unless your previously saved choice is on. Enable it when you want to sync your Library and use iCloud storage. Tap the cloud next to Settings, or choose Settings > iCloud Library, and use Sync with iCloud. Sign in to iCloud on your device and make sure enough iCloud storage is available. Check progress or choose Sync Now on that screen.

How do I restore my Library after reinstalling or changing devices?

Sign in to the same iCloud account and open Playlist Studio while connected. The app restores your last saved sync choice. If it was on, previously synced media, recordings, saved Voice-over mixes, and playlists download automatically; if it was off, enable Sync with iCloud when you want to restore. Only data and choices that reached iCloud can be restored. Editable Recent Sessions are not included. If iCloud is unavailable, local files remain available and transfers wait.

What happens if I turn iCloud sync off?

Library transfers stop, while local data and existing iCloud copies are kept. Your choice is saved separately in your private iCloud database, even while Library sync is off, so it can be remembered after reinstalling or changing devices. A changed choice must reach iCloud first. New changes wait until sync is enabled again; deletions also sync while enabled. iCloud sync does not provide a version history.

Does syncing continue after I leave the app?

Sync may continue for a limited time after you leave the app or lock your device. iOS may also schedule later background opportunities, depending on system conditions. These runs are not continuous or guaranteed. Interrupted sync resumes when you return or iOS allows another run; an incomplete file may need to be transferred again.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items and playlists inside the app. Library deletions also sync while iCloud sync is enabled and available. Deleting a saved Voice-over mix or its Library source removes associated sessions from Recent Sessions; their local source and voice copies are cleaned up the next time you open Voice-over. A session already open in Studio is kept while you work. You can also choose Delete Session in Recent Sessions. Removing an item only from a playlist keeps its session. Other Library items, exported files, and the completed export count are unchanged. If cleanup fails, use Retry Cleanup or reopen Voice-over. Deleting the app removes its local container but does not delete completed iCloud copies. Manage iCloud storage and device backups separately in Apple’s settings.

Free Voice-over export usage

A count of completed Voice-over exports is stored on this device in Keychain and in a private local recovery copy excluded from device backups. These records apply the 10-export free allowance, are separate from Library items and editable sessions, and are not reset when you delete a Library mix or a session. Keychain storage may be retained by the operating system separately from the app’s local container.

Microphone

Playlist Studio requests microphone permission only when you use Record Audio or start recording in Voice-over. Audio is captured, processed, and stored locally. You can deny or revoke access at any time in System Settings. If permission is denied, recording will not work, but the rest of the app remains available.

Background audio and media controls

For local media playback, including the sound from local videos, Playlist Studio may provide title, playback status, progress, and artwork to Apple’s system media services so that Lock Screen, Control Center, headset, and vehicle controls can work. This processing occurs through iOS or iPadOS.
