Optional iCloud sync

Your library, ready for your next device.

Keep your playlists, imported audio and video, and recordings in your private iCloud storage. Sync is on by default unless your previously saved choice is off. You can change it in Settings > iCloud Library.

After reinstalling or changing devices, sign in to the same iCloud account. If your last saved choice was on, previously synced data downloads while the app is open and connected. If it was off, it stays off until you enable it. Only data and choices that finished syncing can be restored.

The cloud next to Settings opens iCloud Library. A blue cloud means sync is on; it is filled when the app has confirmed all current changes are synced. A gray crossed cloud means sync is off. Open iCloud Library to check progress or resolve a sync issue.

Sync needs internet and available iCloud storage. It may continue briefly after you leave the app, and iOS may allow later background runs. These opportunities are not continuous or guaranteed.

Playlist Studio Support

Find quick answers below. If you still need help, email us and include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue.

What can I add to my Library?

Use Import Audio or Import Video to choose files from Files, or Record Audio to make a recording. Add saved items to one or more playlists using Add from Library.

Does Playlist Studio work without internet?

Yes. Import files already available on your device, record audio, organize playlists, and play locally saved audio and video offline. iCloud sync and purchasing or restoring Unlimited Library need internet. Files stored only with a cloud provider must first be downloaded.

Which video files can I import?

Compatible MP4, MOV, and M4V files. Playback depends on the file’s codec and support on your device. Video is copied into private app storage without re-encoding. Protected or unsupported files cannot be imported.

Can audio and video play in the background?

Imported audio and personal recordings can continue in the background and use Lock Screen controls. Video playback pauses when the app moves to the background.

How does Repeat work?

Playlists start over after the last item. You can also repeat the current item. A playlist or Library with a single item repeats it automatically.

What is included for free?

The free Library supports up to 25 items in total: audio, video, and recordings. Unlimited Library removes that fixed limit through a one-time purchase, subject to device storage and normal technical limits.

How do I restore Unlimited Library?

Open Unlimited Library and choose Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID that made the purchase and an internet connection. The purchase does not renew. Restoring this purchase is separate from restoring your files through iCloud.

Where are my files and playlists stored?

Imported copies, recordings, and playlists are stored in Playlist Studio’s private storage on your device. With Sync with iCloud enabled and available, the Library also syncs to your private iCloud storage. Appearance and playback preferences remain local.

What does the iCloud button mean?

Tap the cloud next to Settings to open iCloud Library. Blue means sync is on; a filled blue cloud means the app has confirmed all current changes are synced. A gray crossed cloud means sync is off. The icon alone does not explain every connection issue; check the status inside iCloud Library.

How do I enable iCloud sync?

Sync is on by default unless your previously saved choice is off. Tap the cloud next to Settings, or choose Settings > iCloud Library, and use Sync with iCloud. Sign in to iCloud on your device and make sure enough iCloud storage is available. Check progress or choose Sync Now on that screen.

How do I restore my Library after reinstalling or changing devices?

Sign in to the same iCloud account and open Playlist Studio while connected. The app restores your last saved sync choice. If it was on, previously synced audio, video, recordings, and playlists download automatically; if it was off, enable Sync with iCloud when you want to restore. Only data and choices that reached iCloud can be restored. If iCloud is unavailable, local files remain available and transfers wait.

What happens if I turn iCloud sync off?

Library transfers stop, while local data and existing iCloud copies are kept. Your choice is saved separately in your private iCloud database, even while Library sync is off, so it can be remembered after reinstalling or changing devices. A changed choice must reach iCloud first. New changes wait until sync is enabled again; deletions also sync while enabled. iCloud sync does not provide a version history.

Does syncing continue after I leave the app?

Sync may continue for a limited time after you leave the app or lock your device. iOS may also schedule later background opportunities, depending on system conditions. These runs are not continuous or guaranteed. Interrupted sync resumes when you return or iOS allows another run; an incomplete file may need to be transferred again.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items and playlists inside the app. Deletions also sync while iCloud sync is enabled and available. Deleting the app removes its local container but does not delete completed iCloud copies. Manage the app’s iCloud storage and device backups separately in Apple’s settings. You can revoke microphone permission in System Settings.

2. Information processed on your device

The following information may be created, selected, or stored locally in the app’s private container:

Files

You choose files through Apple’s system file picker. Playlist Studio accesses only the items you select. Selected audio may be converted on the device to M4A/AAC and copied into the app’s private storage so it can be organized and played. Microphone

Playlist Studio requests microphone permission only when you use the recording feature. Audio is captured, processed, and stored locally. You can deny or revoke access at any time in System Settings. If permission is denied, recording will not work, but the rest of the app remains available.

Background audio and media controls

For local audio playback, Playlist Studio may provide title, playback status, progress, and artwork to Apple’s system media services so that Lock Screen, Control Center, headset, and vehicle controls can work. This processing occurs through iOS or iPadOS.

iCloud Library

Library data and local audio and video files sync to your private iCloud database, associated with the iCloud account signed in on your device. This uses your iCloud storage and requires an internet connection. A local copy remains available for offline playback.

After reinstalling or changing devices, sign in to the same iCloud account. The app restores your last sync choice saved in iCloud: if it was on, your synced Library downloads automatically while the app is open; if it was off, it stays off until you enable it in Settings > iCloud Library. Only changes that finished syncing can be restored.

Sync with iCloud is on by default unless your previously saved choice is off. Your sync choice is stored separately in your private iCloud database, including when library sync is off, so it can be remembered after reinstalling or changing devices. A changed choice must reach iCloud before it can be restored elsewhere.

If iCloud is unavailable, your local Library remains available and library transfers wait. You can turn it on or off and check sync status anytime in Settings > iCloud Library. Turning it off stops syncing without deleting the local Library or copies already saved in iCloud.

Sync may continue for a limited time after you leave the app or lock the device, and iOS may schedule later background opportunities. These opportunities are not continuous or guaranteed. Interrupted transfers resume on a later permitted run; an incomplete file may need to be transferred again.

Deletions also sync while sync is enabled and available. iCloud synchronization is separate from device backup and does not provide version history or guarantee recovery of unsynced or deleted items. Manage the app’s iCloud storage in device Settings.
